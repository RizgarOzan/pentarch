# Hook formulas

Use the formula name exactly as written here in `li.py log add --hook`, so stats group correctly. Each entry: shape, then a neutral example. All are conventions; which ones work for a given user is what the log is for.

1. **Contrarian Take**: "Most people do X. I stopped, and Y happened."
2. **Number Reveal**: a specific number up front. "40 applications, 0 replies. Then one change."
3. **Mistake Confession**: "I did X for a year. It was wrong. Here is what I do now."
4. **Before/After**: two states, one line each. "Before: 3 hours per cover letter. After: 20 minutes."
5. **List Promise**: "5 things I wish I knew before my first game jam."
6. **Insider Secret**: "What recruiters see in the first 6 seconds of a CV."
7. **Callout**: name the reader. "If you are applying to junior roles this month, read this."
8. **Question Trap**: a question with an unexpected answer. "Why did my worst project get me the interview?"
9. **Story Cold Open**: start mid-scene. "The build broke 10 minutes before the demo."
10. **Receipt**: a result with proof. "Load time went from 4.2s to 1.1s. Screenshot in the comments."
11. **Myth Bust**: "No, you do not need a degree for this. Here is what you do need."
12. **Comparison**: "Two juniors, same skills. One got the offer. The difference was one habit."
13. **Permission Slip**: "You are allowed to apply with a half-finished portfolio."
14. **Pattern Interrupt**: a short line that does not fit the feed. "I deleted my portfolio."
15. **Warning**: "Do this before your next interview, or you will lose a week."
16. **Good vs Great**: "A good README explains. A great README makes you want to clone."
17. **Time Anchor**: "One year ago I could not write a shader. Today a client paid for one."
18. **Unpopular Rule**: "Rule I follow that nobody likes: one project, finished, beats five started."
19. **Curiosity Gap**: "The feedback that changed how I write code had nothing to do with code."
20. **Walk-Away**: "I turned down the offer. Here is why it was the right call."
21. **Direct Value**: no story, just the thing. "Free tool that checks your CV against a job ad: (in comments)."

Pick by pillar: authority leans on 2, 10, 16, 21; narrative on 3, 4, 9, 17, 20; community on 7, 8, 13.

A hook that is a label ("Thoughts on junior hiring") is not a formula. Rewrite it with one of the 21 before drafting the body.
